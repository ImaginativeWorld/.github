**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts or sign-in, shows no ads, and does not use analytics or advertising identifiers.

The Application does not gather any information about the location of your mobile device.

The information you create while using the Application is stored only on your device, in the Application's local storage. The Service Provider never receives it:

*   Your profile choices (name, theme, accent colour and language)
*   Your likes, saves, comments and reflections

If you turn on reminders, the Application schedules notifications locally on your device. They are not sent from a server.

If you choose to share a card or text, the content you share goes to the app you pick. The Application does not share anything on its own.

**Third Party Access**

The Application does not send your information to third parties.
